03 · The Brief

The challenge

The channel had a strong creator and a winning niche, but growth was inconsistent and revenue leaned almost entirely on low-RPM Indian AdSense. The goal:

  • Turn one-off reviews into a branded, serialized franchise.

04 · Content Strategy

The strategy I built

A franchise model plus a data-driven product-selection filter — so every upload reinforces the brand and is engineered for the Shorts feed.

The franchise format

I turned standalone reviews into the numbered "Paisa Wasool or Paisa Barbaad" series. Serialized episodes create habit-watching — viewers who see Ep 87 come back for Ep 88 — and a binary verdict (worth it / wasted) bakes a hook into every title.

Product-selection filter

I scored every candidate product against four signals before we filmed:

  • Scroll-stopping? Visually unusual enough to halt the feed.
  • Relatable price? Affordable for a mass Indian audience.
  • Demonstrable in 60s? Clear, satisfying payoff on camera.
  • Buy intent? Will viewers want to purchase after watching?

05 · Scriptwriting

The 5-phase script template

Every Short follows a tight, repeatable structure I wrote — calibrated so the full review (unbox → demo → verdict) lands in under a minute.

  1. 0–3s

    The Hook

    Open mid-reaction with the product in hand and a provocative claim or question. The single job: stop the scroll.

  2. 3–15s

    Unboxing & setup

    Show packaging, name the price and source. Establishes credibility and relatability fast.

  3. 15–45s

    The Demonstration

    The core payoff — product in action, delivering the satisfaction viewers came for.

  4. 45–55s

    The Verdict

    Honest call: Paisa Wasool or Paisa Barbaad. The binary keeps viewers watching for the answer.

  5. 55–60s

    Call to action

    Drive to the Linktree storefront to buy, plus subscribe / like — closing the loop to revenue.

06 · Video Editing

The editing workflow I ran

Editing choices engineered for the exact signals the Shorts algorithm rewards: swipe-through, loop rate, and early retention.

⏱️

3-second hook cut

Front-load the most striking frame to beat the 50–60% early drop-off.

✂️

2–4s cut rhythm

Rotate face, product close-up, demo, and reaction to prevent viewer fatigue.

💬

Burned-in captions

On-screen text for sound-off viewing — a 15–25% retention lift.

🔁

Loop endings

Final frame flows back into the opening to trigger replays and watch time.
